## Scientific question and prediction
Does a simpler target-only allocation give more reliable tolerance-based ID retention than a mixed target/bridge/global allocation at equal64 proposals under the fixed trainer, while reducing target error? Expected: target-only yields >=20% diagnostic PQ-voltage RMSE reduction with ID tolerance in at least3/4 independent sampling replicates, and its median diagnostic RMSE is no worse than mixed. Treat these as two observable exploratory predictions, not equivalence or component causality. Fewer than3 target-only joint passes opposes reliability prediction; higher target-only median diagnostic RMSE opposes relative-error prediction. A failed/integrity-invalid experiment does not refute either. Four seeds are not a powered population comparison.
