# P4 scientific comparison boundary

Successor to verified P3. Active scope is contract planning; no new model or
experiment allowance is granted by P3's engineering closeout.

Outcome: one defensible scientific comparison in power-grid sampling, solver
methods or their combination. The current autonomous researcher is the future
Research Lead prototype. It proposes hypotheses and methods; the evaluation
contract constrains claims and resources rather than prescribing its reasoning.

Before an actual P4 run:

1. Record the scientific question, original equations and allowed control
   variables. Separate algorithm, scenario and problem-definition changes.
2. Freeze development and held-out scenario families, split provenance, baseline
   and evaluation rules. Researcher/candidate access must not expose hidden assets.
3. Specify equal-budget comparison, failure denominators, equation validity,
   operational constraints, missing-data treatment and generalization claims.
4. Admit a finite subscription-first model/compute budget, with justified limits
   and resumable exceedance receipts under AGENTS.md. P3's 180-second acceptance
   budget is not a long-research default.
5. Verify that the chosen workload fits the admitted candidate/tool profile. Do
   not silently enlarge limits, relax evaluation rules or switch to paid APIs.
6. Run autonomous hypothesis/method iterations, retain negative evidence and
   independently replicate promising, null and adverse outcomes as appropriate.

P3 established one model-authored fixed-case method and reproducible material,
not comparative improvement or novelty. P4 must not use its same-case replication
as held-out validation. Multi-Lead coordination and delegated Workers remain P5;
Capstone's separate preparation/acceptance lane remains independent.
